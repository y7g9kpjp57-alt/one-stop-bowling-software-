🍎 iPhone / iPad

Use Safari for the easiest Home Screen setup.

1
Open the software in SafariTap the blue button above.
2
Tap the Share buttonIn Safari, tap the square with the upward arrow.
3
Choose “Add to Home Screen”You may need to scroll down the Share menu.
4
Tap “Add”The One Stop Bowling Software icon will appear on your Home Screen.

🤖 Android

Google Chrome is recommended.

1
Open the software in ChromeTap the blue button above.
2
Open the Chrome menuTap the three dots in the top-right corner.
3
Choose “Add to Home screen” or “Install app”The wording can vary slightly by Android version.
4
Tap “Add” / “Install”The software will appear on the Home Screen.

Joining a tournament

If an organiser gives you a tournament code, open the software and tap Open Code. Enter the code to view the tournament. Signed-in scorers can request scoring access from the organiser.

Public users can view tournament details without scoring permissions.
